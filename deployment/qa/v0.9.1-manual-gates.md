## Human review

### 1. Release truth and audience clarity

- Confirm the root page, standalone aliases, immutable UI handoff, Site Manifest, Build Card, implementation notes, and Color Delivery record resolve the same release tuple.
- Confirm audience-facing page content contains no placeholder, TODO, debug output, validator message, unresolved approval workflow, local path, or unexplained package/schema residue.
- Confirm any real limitation is specific, plain-language, and adjacent to what it limits.
- Confirm the page does not imply that the owner-approved Design System automatically certifies this downstream artifact.

### 2. Learning arc and implementation examples

- Confirm the page makes the difference between a retained principle and a v0.9.1 implementation improvement obvious before the reader reaches the extended library.
- Confirm the constructive cases cover shared/product layer separation, initial/visible/hydrated parity, calm navigation with truthful CTA and fail-open motion, and cross-format semantic parity.
- Confirm the compact analytical case treats schema/release/unit/grain incompatibility as a valid result.
- Confirm the rejected motion case is static, bounded, and paired with a visible recovery.
- Confirm every new case visibly says in Thai and English that it is conceptual and not product evidence.

### 3. Shared and product-specific evidence boundaries

- Confirm Locale Insight is used only at portfolio, methodology, and product-architecture level across Land, Location, and Living.
- Confirm named-product data, capability, permission, workflow, score, model, audience, voice, and claim remain in a named-product layer.
- Confirm no CityMETER, CityWiki, CityChat, ijji, municipality, retail, or F&B example is presented as portfolio-wide truth.
- Confirm cross-product or cross-city comparison uses compatible schema/release/unit/grain or displays incompatibility beside the comparison.

### 4. Navigation, focus, and direct targets

- At 1440 and 1024 CSS pixels, confirm the desktop header exposes no more than four visible controls including brand.
- At 390, 360, and 320 CSS pixels, confirm the mobile header exposes no more than two visible controls including brand.
- Confirm every direct interactive header, menu, and bookmark target is at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
- Confirm the menu is a semantic button with `aria-expanded`, opens and closes without focus loss, closes with Escape, and returns focus to the opener.
- Confirm brand page-current state and side-bookmark location-current state are distinct.
- Confirm each deep link lands below the sticky header and the target remains visible and focusable.

### 5. CTA and motion

- Confirm each CTA starts with a clear verb, names one truthful destination/outcome, remains untruncated, and has no hidden side effect.
- Confirm the discovery cue is finite and occurs no more than once per page load.
- Confirm H1, first answer, first proof, primary action, deep-link target, and focus target are never hidden behind motion.
- Confirm source HTML contains the final meaning and remains fully usable with JavaScript disabled.
- Confirm `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and missing observer support show the final state immediately.
- Confirm motion never gates evidence, action, reading order, or focus recovery.

### 6. Icon and selected state

- Confirm every icon uses the governed Material Symbols Rounded subset at `FILL 0, wght 300, GRAD 0, opsz 24` in every state.
- Confirm selected state uses surface, container, and label changes; no filled glyph appears.
- Confirm icon-only controls have a locale-correct accessible name and a 44 × 44 circular target.

### 7. Thai, English, and layout resilience

- Confirm Thai and English communicate the same governed meaning without treating one global Thai display line-height as safe at every size.
- Inspect Thai, Latin, and mixed-script fixtures at their actual sizes, outputs, and weights for clipping, combining marks, baseline drift, and false bold.
- Review both locales at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 CSS pixels, at 130% Thai text and 200% zoom.
- Confirm no root horizontal overflow, clipped action, unreadable table, or overlapping fixed navigation.

### 8. Color Atlas preservation

- Confirm the Color Atlas remains a first-class destination from the hero, header/menu, retained-foundations callout, and library.
- In Light and Dark, confirm 17 foundation pairs, 7 semantic states, 7 shared atmosphere gradients, 3 asset-only motif records, 4 product-gradient families/8 theme specimens, 10 categorical pairs, 18 analytical scale records/738 LUT cells/378 class cells, 8 map pairs, 8 opacity values, and 6 depth roles.
- Confirm the concise sampler still shows all six sequential and three diverging families at exact 5/7/9 classes.
- Confirm Color Set `color-srgb-05` values and semantic ownership remain unchanged and the v0.9.0 immutable Color Set baseline remains byte-identical.
- Confirm analytical scales retain their recorded source/version boundary and never become named-product evidence by implication.

### 9. Standalone and delivery parity

- Open the latest and immutable v0.9.1 standalones with network access blocked; confirm embedded fonts, logo, content images, language, theme, menu, disclosures, and the complete Color Atlas remain usable.
- Confirm the hosted root and latest standalone are equivalent after expected standalone embedding and canonical-link transformations.
- Confirm latest and immutable UI standalones are equivalent after normalizing only their build-channel marker.
- Before cross-device color review, record DS version, Color Set, artifact build, resolved theme, token SHA-256, scale SHA-256, and artifact SHA-256. Compare only matching immutable bytes in explicit Light, then explicit Dark.
- After deployment, verify the returned root and every critical asset byte-for-byte against the merged source commit.

### 10. Accessibility and recovery

- Complete the keyboard route from skip link through header/menu, implementation examples, disclosures, Color Atlas, resources, and return paths.
- Confirm visible focus, logical order, locale-correct names, 44 × 44 direct targets, and stable state under Back/Forward restoration.
- Verify supported screen readers in Thai and English, with reduced motion, high zoom, no JavaScript, offline standalone, and interrupted/restored history.
- Confirm every interactive error or unavailable state preserves a clear edit, retry, cancel, close, copy, or return route appropriate to the action.

Product capability, product evidence, city truth, or cross-product comparability requires its own approved, compatible, and current evidence release.

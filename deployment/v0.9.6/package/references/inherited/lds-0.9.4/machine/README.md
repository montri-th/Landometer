## Boundaries that did not move

Design System governs form, interaction, accessibility and format; it creates no product fact, no publication permission and no traction claim. Motifs are decoration: they encode no data, evidence, state or permission, and identity of record remains the approved PNG files. Portfolio language stays product-neutral across Land, Location and Living; product stings and their meanings stay inside their product DS add-ons. Audience output remains resolved-only under OUTPUT-CLARITY-01: nothing in this package's status may appear on an audience-facing surface. Do not edit this package in place; a byte change is a new machine-package identifier (v0.9.4-mp2 or later) and a new freeze.
